## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Don't over-optimize** - Write for humans first, search engines second
2. **Don't keyword stuff** - Keywords should feel natural
3. **Don't skip testing** - Actually verify your changes work
4. **Don't publish and forget** - Track what resonates, iterate
5. **Don't compare your beginning** to someone else's middle - Stay patient
